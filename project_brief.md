# My agent: travel-concierge
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps travelers discover destinations and plan tailored itineraries with a catalog of travel packages, local attractions, and accommodations.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User travel preferences, past trips, budget range, and dietary/mobility needs
- Tools: Destination search, weather and attraction lookup, itinerary generator
- Catalog/UI: Travel packages, hotel listings, and day-by-day itineraries rendered as cards and tables
- Image gen: Destination postcards and scenic attraction previews
- Video gen: Scenic destination video clips using gemini-omni-flash-preview
- Sandbox: Currency conversions, budget calculations, and trip duration math

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for currency/budget calculations, Google Maps / Places integration
